How it works

  1. Paste it. Copy the text or email and paste it into the box.
  2. We look for warning signs. Pressure, threats, strange payments and fake links are checked on your device.
  3. You decide. You get a score, the reasons in plain words, and what to do next.

    If you think you’ve been scammed

    • If you shared card or bank details, call your bank right away.
    • Report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov and, for internet crimes, ic3.gov.
    • Tell family or a friend. Scams work best in secret, and shame helps nobody.

    How the checking works

    Two layers work together, both inside the page:

    • Rules. checks look for pressure, threats, payment requests, private-information requests, and fake or lookalike links. Each finding comes with a plain-language reason.
    • A small word model. A naive Bayes classifier learns from short example messages when the page loads. It adds a gentle nudge to the score (20%), and the rules carry the rest.

    This is a helper, not a guarantee. It can miss new scams and flag real messages. When in doubt, contact the company using a number you already trust.
