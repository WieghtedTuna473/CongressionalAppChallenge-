    Read this before trusting the numbers

    • The test messages were never used to train the word model, but they were written by the developer, so results are likely better than in the real world.
    • Weights were set by judgment and adjusted on a handful of examples. The test set was scored once, with no changes afterward.
    • “Warning” means Be careful or Likely a scam. “Likely scam” is a stricter label, so some scams only reach Be careful.
    • New scam styles can slip through. Treat Second Look as a second opinion, not a guarantee.

    Help in Georgia

    • Georgia Attorney General’s Consumer Protection Division: consumer.georgia.gov. Call (404) 651-8600, or toll-free in Georgia (800) 869-1123.
    • That site also has a Consumer Protection Guide for Older Adults.

    Before you click, tap or pay

    1. Pause. Scams rush you.
    2. Don’t use links or phone numbers in the message.
    3. Call a number you already trust.
    4. Never pay with gift cards, wire transfers or crypto.
    5. Tell someone you trust.

    Report scams: reportfraud.ftc.gov. In Georgia: (800) 869-1123.

    If you think you’ve been scammed

    • If you shared card or bank details, call your bank right away.
    • Report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov and, for internet crimes, ic3.gov.
    • Tell family or a friend. Scams work best in secret, and shame helps nobody.

    How the checking works

    Two layers work together, both inside the page:

    • Rules. checks look for pressure, threats, payment requests, private-information requests, and fake or lookalike links. Each finding comes with a plain-language reason.
    • A small word model. A naive Bayes classifier learns from short example messages when the page loads. It adds a gentle nudge to the score (20%), and the rules carry the rest.

    The only thing this page remembers is your text size and contrast choice, stored in your browser. Your messages are never stored.

    This is a helper, not a guarantee. It can miss new scams and flag real messages. When in doubt, contact the company using a number you already trust.
